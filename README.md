RetailIQ: Customer Shopping Behavior Analytics 📊🛍️

RetailIQ is a complete end-to-end Data Analytics & Business Intelligence Project designed to analyze customer purchasing behavior, revenue trends, product performance, and customer retention patterns using Python, SQL, and Power BI.

This project transforms raw retail transaction data into actionable business insights that help businesses improve:

Customer retention
Revenue growth
Marketing strategy
Product positioning
Discount optimization
🚀 Project Highlights

✅ 3,900+ customer transactions analyzed
✅ End-to-end analytics workflow
✅ Advanced Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
✅ SQL-driven business insights
✅ Interactive Power BI dashboard
✅ Customer segmentation analysis
✅ Revenue & discount behavior tracking
✅ Business recommendations for growth

📌 Business Problem

Retail businesses generate massive amounts of transactional data but often struggle to answer critical questions such as:

Which customers generate the highest revenue?
Are discounts increasing profit or reducing margins?
Which products perform best?
Which customer segments should be targeted?
How effective are subscription programs?
Which age groups contribute the most revenue?

RetailIQ solves these problems through data-driven analytics and visualization.
